Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information KonKo Rush ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Kudjo Ewusi Mensah ("we").

In short

  • The game shows ads, served through Unity LevelPlay. Ads are personalised only if you allow it.
  • You can turn off personalised ads, or opt out of the sale or sharing of your data, in Settings at any time.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Online races and leaderboards use an anonymous player ID and the driver name you choose.
  • You can delete your online account and leaderboard entries in Settings at any time.

Information on your device

The game saves your progress (cars, credits, upgrades and race results) and your settings on your device. We can't see this information, and it is deleted when you delete the game.

Online races

When you open online play, the game signs in to Unity Gaming Services, run by Unity Technologies, to make and join rooms. This uses:

  • An anonymous player ID, created by Unity's sign-in service. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple account.
  • Your driver name, which you type yourself. Other players in your room see it above your car.
  • Race data, such as car positions and items used, sent between players during a race so everyone sees the same race.

Unity processes this information to provide the online rooms. See Unity's privacy policy. If you never open online play, none of this is created.

Leaderboards

The game has weekly and all-time leaderboards, run on Supabase, a hosted database service with servers in London. When the game starts, it signs in to Supabase with an anonymous account. There's no sign-up and no email or password. The game sends:

  • An anonymous account ID, created by Supabase. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple account, and it is never shown to other players.
  • Your driver name, which other players see on the leaderboards next to a random public ID.
  • Your results: best lap times (with the circuit, car and mode), total career stars and your places in online races.
  • The platform and game version, so results can be checked and problems fixed.

Driver names are checked against a list of offensive words. Any player can report a name, and a name reported by three different players is hidden everywhere. You can delete your online account, with all of its leaderboard entries, in Settings > Gameplay > Delete online account.

Advertising

The game is free to play and shows ads: an optional video you can choose to watch for extra credits, and an occasional full-screen ad between races. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, run by Unity Technologies, and its advertising partners (such as Unity Ads).

To choose, show and measure ads, LevelPlay and its partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking when the game asks).
  • Device and usage information, such as device model, operating system, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, and which ads you saw or tapped.

Your choices about ads:

  • On iPhone, the game asks whether to allow tracking. If you say no, your advertising identifier isn't shared. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. Change your answer at any time in the game's Settings > Gameplay > Personalised ads.
  • Everywhere, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for advertising in Settings > Gameplay > Sell or share my data.

If you decline, you still see ads, just less relevant ones. See Unity's privacy policy for players for how Unity and LevelPlay handle this information.

What we don't collect

The game doesn't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or payment details. We don't run our own analytics.

Children

The game is suitable for players aged 9 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Driver names are shown to other players in online rooms and on the leaderboards, so players shouldn't use their real name.

Your choices

  • Change your driver name at any time in the online menu.
  • Delete your online account and leaderboard entries in Settings > Gameplay > Delete online account.
  • Turn off personalised ads, or opt out of the sale or sharing of your data, in Settings > Gameplay.
  • Delete the game to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Email us to ask about, or delete, information linked to your player ID.

Changes

If the game starts using information in new ways, such as accounts, purchases or analytics, we'll update this policy first and change the date above.
